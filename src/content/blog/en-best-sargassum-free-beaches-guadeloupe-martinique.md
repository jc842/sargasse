# Where to Swim: Finding Sargassum-Free Beaches in Guadeloupe & Martinique

A trip to the French Caribbean should never be spoiled by beach stranding alerts. Thanks to the islands' volcanic geography, vast sections of the coastline are naturally shielded from Atlantic drift.

---

## Guadeloupe: The Best Protected Spots
1. **Plage de Grande Anse (Deshaies)**: Located on the northwest coast of Basse-Terre, this sweeping golden-sand beach never experiences sargassum strandings.
2. **Plage de Malendure (Bouillante)**: Famed for sea turtles and the Cousteau Marine Reserve, completely sheltered by the Caribbean sea.
3. **Plage de la Caravelle (Sainte-Anne lagoon)**: While on Grande-Terre, its shallow coral barrier and western bay curve keep its turquoise lagoon clear.
4. **Îlets Pigeon & Les Saintes (Pain de Sucre)**: Stunning calm bays with deep turquoise drop-offs.

## Martinique: The Best Protected Spots
1. **Les Anses d’Arlet (Anse Noire & Anse Dufour)**: Nestled in the southwest Caribbean nook, protected by steep volcanic headlands.
2. **Plage des Salines (Sainte-Anne - Southern Tip)**: Usually protected during standard east-northeast swell patterns.
3. **Anse Couleuvre (Le Prêcheur)**: Untamed black volcanic sand at the foot of Mount Pelée, facing pure Caribbean waters.
